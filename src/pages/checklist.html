About this list

  • Move-In / Move-Out assumes an empty home, so furniture items do not apply.
  • Baseboards, window sills and ceiling fans are dusted in Standard and wiped in Deep and Move-In / Move-Out.
  • Microwave is cleaned inside and out in every tier. Fridge steps up from 'outside' to 'inside & out' in Deep and Move-In / Move-Out.
  • Included in Move-In / Move-Out but paid add-ons in Standard and Deep: inside oven, inside cabinets, interior windows.
  • Making beds and changing bedding is a paid add-on, not part of any tier.
  • Garage, outdoor structures, pet home, and heavy-duty condition are priced separately from the booking form.

Good to know

  • Before we arrive: clear dishes and personal items from surfaces, secure pets, and empty the fridge and cabinets if you booked inside cleaning.
  • Not included in any tier: behind or under heavy appliances and furniture, mold or biohazard cleanup, exterior windows (unless booked), garage and outdoor structures.
  • Condition: the final price is confirmed on arrival based on the home's condition (1-10 scale; heavy-duty is a separate fee) and shown to you before work begins.
  • If a Standard clean needs Deep-level work, you choose: approve the new price, upgrade to Deep, or keep the Standard scope. Price changes over $20 need your approval; if you decline, you can cancel that visit at no charge.
