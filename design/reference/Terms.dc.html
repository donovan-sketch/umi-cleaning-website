8. Booking, Estimates, and Confirmed Pricing

The price shown when you book is an estimate based on the details you provide: home size, bedrooms, bathrooms, condition, pets, add-ons, and frequency. Accurate details keep your estimate accurate.

When the service provider arrives, they confirm the scope of work and the final price.

  • If the confirmed price is within $20 of your estimate, the service proceeds at the confirmed price.
  • If it differs by more than $20, we will ask you to approve the new price before work begins.
  • If you don't approve it, you may cancel that visit with no cancellation fee.

Add-ons you request on the day are added to the confirmed price.

9. Payment and Card Authorization

A valid payment card is required to book. Your card is saved when you book but is not charged at that time.

By booking, you authorize UMI Cleaning, through its payment processor, to charge your card on file for:

  • the confirmed price of each completed service, charged when the service is completed;
  • any fees under Section 11 (late cancellation or lockout); and
  • any add-ons you approved.

If a charge fails, we will notify you and may retry the card. We may pause future bookings until the balance is paid. Tips are optional and go entirely to your service provider. We do not store your full card number.

Pay only through UMI Cleaning. Never pay a service provider directly by cash, app, or check. Payments made outside UMI Cleaning are not covered by these Terms, our Re-Clean Guarantee, or our damage claims process.

10. Recurring Service

If you choose weekly, every-other-week, every-three-weeks, or monthly service, you are signing up for a recurring plan. Your plan continues, and your card is charged after each completed visit, until you cancel. Your booking confirmation shows your frequency and the estimated price per visit.

  • Recurring discounts apply while your plan is active. If you cancel the plan or skip enough visits to change your frequency, later visits are priced at the rate for the frequency you actually use.
  • We will notify you by email or text at least 14 days before any change to your recurring price.
  • You can cancel your plan at any time by emailing hello@umicleaning.com, by replying to any booking text, or through your booking account. Cancellation is free. It applies to every visit scheduled more than 48 hours after you cancel. A visit less than 48 hours away is subject to the fees in Section 11.

11. Cancellations, Rescheduling, and Lockouts

We hold a service provider's time for your booking, so short-notice changes cost us.

When you cancel or reschedule Fee
48 hours or more before the visit No charge
24 to 48 hours before the visit 25% of the scheduled price
Less than 24 hours before the visit 50% of the scheduled price

Fees. Any cancellation or rescheduling fee in the table above is charged to your card.

Rescheduling limit. You may reschedule each visit for free up to two times with 48+ hours' notice. A third reschedule of the same visit is treated as a cancellation.

Lockout. If the service provider can't get in or safely begin work, we will hold the visit for 30 minutes after arrival while we try to reach you. If the issue isn't resolved by then, the visit is cancelled and 50% of the scheduled price is charged.

If we must cancel or reschedule your booking, we will tell you as early as we can and you will not be charged.

Utilities. Running water and working electricity are required. If they're unavailable, the lockout terms in Section 11 apply.

Pets. Please secure any pet that may be aggressive, anxious, or likely to escape. Service providers may need to open doors while working, and we are not responsible for a pet that gets out of an area you did not secure.

Valuables. Please put away cash, jewelry, important documents, and fragile or high-value items before your service.

Not included. Outdoor areas, biohazards, junk removal, moving items over 25 lbs, ladder work, or cleaning around loose medications, cannabis, cash, or jewelry left out (those areas will be skipped). If a visit is stopped for unsafe conditions at the home, the late-cancellation fee in Section 11 may apply.

Photos. Service providers may take photos of service areas to document completed work or resolve an issue. Photos are never used publicly without your written permission. To opt out, tell us in writing before your service.
